Frequently Asked Questions

Ask Us
Do you sell to individual customers or only to shops?

Both. Shops and resellers get wholesale pricing on bulk orders, and individual customers can buy single units.

Which cities do you deliver to?

We're based in Lahore and deliver by courier across Pakistan. Delivery time and charges depend on your city and are confirmed with your order.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer, cash on delivery, JazzCash and Easypaisa.

Do your products come with a warranty?

Warranty terms depend on the product and how it was sourced. We confirm them clearly with every quote, before you pay.

How do I get your wholesale price list?

Send us an enquiry using the form below and select "Shop owner / Reseller". We'll share our current stock and wholesale prices.

Can you source a model that isn't listed?

Often, yes. Tell us the model and quantity and we'll check availability with our local and import suppliers.
